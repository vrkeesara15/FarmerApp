# A43 Result (scouting)

Surface: Field agent app "Mitra" (Android, 360x800 dp), Light and Sunlight themes.
Requirements: FA-019 (attach the result to farmer and crop plot; result stored with confidence and model version), FA-020 (order for the farmer), SPEC 3.4, DX-005, DX-007..DX-009. Derived from SPEC 13.2 "A43 Result: high-confidence variant and provisional variant; TreatmentCards; ProductCards ranked with OwnBrandBadge; "Create order for farmer"".

Implementation: `ScoutResultScreen` (`apps/mobile/apps/agent/lib/screens/scouting/scouting_screens.dart`) over the shared `DiagnosisResultView` + `DiagnosisActionBar`.

## Layout

- Header "For Ramesh Reddy · Cotton" (`scout-attached`): the farmer and the crop plot the result belongs to.
- The shared result (see F25): headline, `DiagnosisResultCard` with the band in words and the symptom checklist, Organic / Chemical `TreatmentCard`s (dose per acre and per 15 L tank, PHI, PPE, safety text), ranked `ProductCard`s with `OwnBrandBadge`, price and stock at the store, cultural practices, F27-style updated banner and earlier answers.
- Footer "Model ondevice-v1.0 · corpus v7" (`scout-provenance`) — the stored model and corpus versions (DX-005).

## Variants

| Variant | Actions |
|---|---|
| High confidence (`can_buy`) | **Create order for farmer** (recommended SKU) in the bar and on every in-stock product card, **Call farmer** |
| Provisional | Dashed card, "Our expert will confirm within 24 hours", treatments shown, **no buy / order action anywhere**, **Call farmer**, **Ask an expert** |
| Out of scope | F28 card, **Ask an expert** |

"Create order for farmer" starts the officer cart for the farmer at the diagnosis' store and adds the SKU with its `label_claim_id` (`CartNotifier.addRecommended`); A51 → A52 → the `order` op carries `lines: [{sku_id, qty, label_claim_id}]`.

## A22 Diagnoses tab

`FarmerDiagnosesList`: the farmer's scouting results (and the farmer's own crop checks that reached this phone through the sync feed), newest first: problem name or status, crop, ConfidenceBand, time; tap → A43. Empty: "No diagnoses yet" + **New diagnosis**.
