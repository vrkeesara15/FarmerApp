# C81 Campaign builder

Surface: Web console (1440x900, responsive check 1024x768), Light and Dark. Route `/campaigns/new` (`?id=` edits a draft, `?from=segment` seeds the segment from C32). Owners, directors, tenant admins.
Requirements: WC-008 (segment builder with live count, consent-only audiences, channel, message per language, schedule, holdout), NF-013 / DPDP (marketing consent).

Implementation: `apps/console/components/growth/CampaignBuilder.tsx`, `SegmentBuilder.tsx`, `PhonePreview.tsx`. Data: `POST /v1/campaigns/segment-count` (live), `POST /v1/campaigns`, `PATCH /v1/campaigns/{id}`, `POST /v1/campaigns/{id}/schedule`. Validation: `validateDraft()` in `lib/campaigns.ts`.

## Layout

1. Header "New campaign" — "Choose who receives it, write the message and schedule the send." — with a back link to C80.
2. **Basics**: Campaign name, Channel (SMS / WhatsApp / App notification), Conversion window in days (default 14) with its explanation.
3. **Who receives it** (`SegmentBuilder`): crops, crop stage, districts, languages (checkbox lists); bought in the last N days + minimum spend, has not bought in N days, visited / not visited in N days, app user, used the app in the last N days; "Clear segment".
4. **Consented audience** panel — the heart of the screen:
   - empty segment: "Pick at least one criterion to count the audience." (no number is ever guessed);
   - otherwise the live line, always naming the number as consented: "120 farmers with marketing consent will receive this." and, when consent excluded anyone, "…31 matching farmers are left out because they never granted marketing consent or withdrew it."; a segment nobody consented to reads "No farmer with marketing consent matches.";
   - a permanent note: "Consent is always applied and cannot be switched off. It is checked again for every batch at send time, so a farmer who withdraws between scheduling and sending is skipped."
5. **Message per language**: one textarea per launch language (English is the fallback and is required), with the **phone preview** beside it — a phone frame showing the bubble in the chosen language's script, the channel and the tenant name, plus "1 SMS part(s) · 48 characters".
6. **Schedule**: holdout percentage (0–50) with its explanation, Send at (IST, `datetime-local`; empty = as soon as possible), the arms split "95 receive the message, 10 are held back as the control group", and the note "Scheduling freezes the consented audience and the holdout split. A draft can still be edited; a scheduled campaign cannot."
7. Actions: **Save draft**, **Schedule campaign**, Back.

## Validation (client, mirrors the API)

Name >= 3 characters; at least one segment criterion; at least one message with English present; message <= 1000 characters; holdout a whole 0–50; conversion window 1–90 days; a send time in the future (read as IST, not the browser's zone). Errors show inline under the field they belong to after the first save attempt. API errors (`empty_segment`, `dlt_template_missing`, `whatsapp_template_missing`, `invalid_campaign_state`) surface above the actions.
