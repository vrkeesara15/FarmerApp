# F31 Cart and pickup or delivery

Surface: Farmer app "Kisan" (Android, 360x800 dp), Light and Sunlight themes.
Requirements: FM-010 (P0) with NF-010. Derived from SPEC 13.3 (F31), 13.1 components and SPEC 2.4 / 7.

Implementation: `apps/mobile/apps/farmer/lib/screens/orders/cart_screen.dart` (route `/cart`), core `cartProvider`.

## Purpose

Lines with big − / + and the estimated total, and one decision: pick up at the store or home delivery (two tiles).

## Components used

| Component | Notes |
|---|---|
| Line cards | Name, pack × price, `QtyStepper` |
| ChoiceGrid | `kisanPickup` (store icon) / `kisanDelivery` (truck icon) |
| CartBar | Estimated total → F32 |

## Data fields shown

| Field | Source | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Lines / estimate | Cart (catalogue prices; the server prices the order) | 2 × Neem Shakti ₹840.00 |

## Actions and navigation

| Action | Result |
|---|---|
| − / + | Quantity (0 removes) |
| Pickup / delivery | Cart fulfilment |
| Go to pay | F32 |

## States covered

| State | Behaviour |
|---|---|
| Empty | EmptyState with "Buy products" |

## Accessibility and literacy

Every icon is paired with a text label; targets are at least 48 dp; body text 16 sp or larger; the key instruction has an `AudioButton` next to it (NF-009: device TTS in the chosen language behind the `AudioPrompt` seam, "Audio unavailable" when the phone has no voice for it). No text input (NF-010). At most two decisions on the screen.
