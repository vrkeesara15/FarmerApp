# F21 Camera (crop doctor)

Surface: Farmer app "Kisan" (Android, 360x800 dp), Light and Sunlight themes.
Requirements: FM-004 (guided camera: frame the affected leaf, tips on light / focus), DX-001. Derived from SPEC 13.3 "F21 Camera with guide overlay".

Implementation: `apps/mobile/apps/farmer/lib/screens/crop_doctor/crop_camera_screen.dart` (`CropCameraScreen`, route `/crop-doctor/camera`) on the shared `GuidedCameraView` (core).

## Purpose

A live camera (the `camera` plugin, back lens, no audio, flash off) with the `CameraGuideOverlay` on top: the leaf frame, "Place the leaf inside the frame" and the three tip chips. After a refused photo the chip that fixes it is lit and the retake line is shown (blurry → hold steady, dark → good light, no leaf → fill the frame).

## Components used

| Component | Notes |
|---|---|
| GuidedCameraView | Preview + CameraGuideOverlay + counter "Photo n of 3" + 80 dp shutter (`camera-shutter`) |
| CameraGuideOverlay | `tipKey` = the last refused photo's tip |

## Actions

| Action | Result |
|---|---|
| Shutter | Shot → `PhotoProcessor` (1280 px, q80, off the UI thread) → DX-001 gate (`TfliteQualityModel`, Laplacian fallback) → F22 |
| Back | F20 |

## States covered

| State | Behaviour |
|---|---|
| Opening | Black with a spinner |
| Camera refused / missing | "Allow the camera to take a photo of your crop" / "The camera did not open" + Try again |
| Checking the shot | Shutter shows a spinner and is disabled |
| Retake after a refusal | Matching chip lit (warning colour, thicker border), retake line under the frame |
| Sunlight | Scrim chips, white-on-dark text |

## Copy keys
