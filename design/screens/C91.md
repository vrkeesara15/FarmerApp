# C91 Travel allowance approvals

Surface: Web console (1440x900, responsive check 1024x768), Light and Dark. Route `/travel` (SideNav "Travel claims"). Cluster heads decide their own cluster's claims; owners, directors and tenant admins decide any; auditors, accountants and officers read.
Requirements: FA-023 (expense claims with a receipt photo, cluster-head approval, monthly statement), VV-006 (computed km).

Implementation: `apps/console/components/growth/TravelView.tsx` over `GET /v1/travel/claims?status=&officer_id=`, `POST /v1/travel/claims/{id}/approve|reject`, `GET /v1/travel/statement?month=&officer_id=`.

## Layout

1. Header "Travel allowance approvals" — "Expense claims by officer and month, with the km computed from duty tracks next to what was claimed." — with **month**, **officer** and **status** selects, all in the URL (`?month=&officer=&status=`, default status "Awaiting decision").
2. Note: "Computed km comes from the officer's duty tracks for that day (VV-006) and is capped at 150 km. Compare it with the claim before approving."
3. Claims table: officer, claim date, type (Fuel / Bus / Other), **Claimed** (₹), **Computed km** (from the duty tracks; "No duty km recorded for that day." when the day has none), **Day allowance** (the statement's travel allowance for that date), status chip (Awaiting decision / Approved success / Rejected danger) and the Approve / Reject buttons, or "Decided 12 Sep 2026, 10:40".
4. Every row expands into the **receipt photo** (`alt` "Receipt photo for 2026-09-04"), the officer's note and the decision note.
5. Decision dialog: "Approve this claim?" / "Reject this claim?", a note ("The officer sees the decision and your note in their app."); a rejection without a note is refused in the console ("Add a note explaining the rejection.").
6. **Monthly statement** for the chosen officer: km travelled, km after the daily cap, travel allowance, approved claims, **payable**; **Export statement** downloads the per-day CSV (date, sessions, km, km after cap, allowance in rupees). Without an officer: "Pick an officer to see the statement."

## States

Loading: DataTable skeleton. Error: `QueryError` with retry (403 -> no-access state). Empty: "No claims to review" / "Claims submitted by officers in your cluster appear here." API 409 `claim_not_pending` surfaces in the dialog.
