# F25 Result — high confidence

Surface: Farmer app "Kisan" (Android, 360x800 dp), Light and Sunlight themes.
Requirements: FM-005 (problem name, confidence band as words, symptoms to confirm, organic and chemical options, dosage per acre and per tank, safety interval, products at the nearest store with price and stock), FM-006 (one-tap Order / Call my officer / Ask an expert), DX-007..DX-009, NF-002. Derived from SPEC 13.3 F25.

Implementation: `DiagnosisResultScreen` (`apps/mobile/apps/farmer/lib/screens/crop_doctor/diagnosis_result_screen.dart`, route `/crop-doctor/result/<id>`) over the shared `DiagnosisResultView` + `DiagnosisActionBar` (core).

## Layout (top to bottom)

1. Headline for `message_key` ("We found the problem"; confirmed / corrected variants).
2. `DiagnosisResultCard`: the farmer's photo, problem name in the app language (English below it when different), `ConfidenceBand` in words ("High confidence"), "Check these signs" — tappable symptom rows the farmer ticks (48 dp).
3. Organic / Chemical segmented tabs (first non-empty kind selected). Per ranked option (rank 1 first, DX-008): `TreatmentCard` (active ingredient, dose per acre, dose per 15 L tank drawn on a knapsack-tank pictogram, "Wait N days before harvest", PPE pictograms with words, label safety text) then its `ProductCard` (pack, price, `OwnBrandBadge`, "N in stock at FCC Kondapur, 3 km" / out of stock, **Order** button).
4. "Products at FCC Kondapur, 3 km" — the store from `treatments.store`, the distance from the cached nearest-store answer.
5. "Also do this" — cultural practices.
6. AudioButton reading the headline and the problem name.
7. Bottom bar: **Order** (the recommended SKU: best in-stock chemical, else organic) and **Call my officer** (one tap to the dialler; without a number, F62 explains).

## Order (FM-006)

Order puts the SKU **with its `label_claim_id`** in the cart at the diagnosis' store (`CartNotifier.addRecommended`); F31 cart → F32 pay places `POST /v1/orders` with `lines: [{sku_id, qty, label_claim_id}]` (FA-020 checks the claim again) and the order reaches the store queue. Out-of-stock products have the Order button disabled.

## Data

`DiagnosisOut` stored in `sync_docs` (readable offline, NF-002); `can_buy` + not provisional + status answered / confirmed / corrected ⇒ `Diagnosis.showBuy`.
