# F27 Result updated

Surface: Farmer app "Kisan" (Android, 360x800 dp), Light and Sunlight themes.
Requirements: FM-005 ("notifies on update"), DX-006. Derived from SPEC 13.3 "F27 Result updated notification state".

Implementation: `DiagnosisCodec` (core) + `DiagnosisResultScreen`, F10 Home card, F20 list.

## Behaviour

- The agronomist's confirm / correct is a new change of the `diagnosis` entity in the sync feed (and a `diagnosis_reviewed` push once FA-007 delivery exists). When it replaces an earlier answer, the phone keeps that answer in `_local.history` and raises `_local.update_unseen`.
- F10 Home shows "Your result was updated — Crop check updated: <problem>" (`home-diagnosis-updated`); F20 lists the check first with a bell.
- Opening the result shows the banner "Your result was updated" with "Our expert confirmed / changed this result" for this visit, then clears the flag. "Earlier answers" lists what was said before ("Earlier: Anthracnose (fruit rot) · Medium confidence"). A confirmed / corrected result can be bought (F25 rules).
