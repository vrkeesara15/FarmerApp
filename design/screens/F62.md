# F62 My field officer

Surface: Farmer app "Kisan" (Android, 360x800 dp), Light and Sunlight themes.
Requirements: FM-011 (P0). Derived from SPEC 13.3 (F62), 13.1 components and SPEC 2.4 / 7.

Implementation: `apps/mobile/apps/farmer/lib/screens/officer_screen.dart` (route `/officer`).

## Purpose

The officer's card: photo placeholder (initial), name, "Field officer", Call and WhatsApp buttons (`url_launcher`).

## Components used

| Component | Notes |
|---|---|
| Avatar | Initial on a tinted circle (photo later) |
| AppButton | Call (`tel:`), WhatsApp (`https://wa.me/<digits>`) |

## Data fields shown

| Field | Source | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Officer | Profile `officer {name, phone}` (API follow-up: not in `FarmerDetailOut` yet) | Ramesh Kumar · +91 91000 00001 |

## Actions and navigation

| Action | Result |
|---|---|
| Call / WhatsApp | Opens the dialer / WhatsApp; "Could not open" otherwise |

## States covered

| State | Behaviour |
|---|---|
| No phone from the API | "Your field officer", buttons off, `kisanOfficerPhoneMissing` |

## Accessibility and literacy

Every icon is paired with a text label; targets are at least 48 dp; body text 16 sp or larger; the key instruction has an `AudioButton` next to it (NF-009: device TTS in the chosen language behind the `AudioPrompt` seam, "Audio unavailable" when the phone has no voice for it). No text input (NF-010). At most two decisions on the screen.
