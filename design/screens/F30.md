# F30 Product detail (and the shop list)

Surface: Farmer app "Kisan" (Android, 360x800 dp), Light and Sunlight themes.
Requirements: FM-010 (P0) with NF-010. Derived from SPEC 13.3 (F30), 13.1 components and SPEC 2.4 / 7.

Implementation: `apps/mobile/apps/farmer/lib/screens/orders/shop_screen.dart` (route `/shop`: products at the farmer's store by category) and `product_detail_screen.dart` (route `/shop/<skuId>`).

## Purpose

The farmer browses the store's products by category chips (no search box) and opens one: name in their language, brand and pack, price at their store, own-brand badge, stock, and two decisions: how many (big − / +) and "Add to cart".

## Components used

| Component | Notes |
|---|---|
| Category chips | `ChoiceChip` with icon per `CatalogCategory` plus All |
| ProductCard | Name, pack, price, own brand, stock; tap → detail |
| CartBar | Items and estimated total → F31 |
| QtyStepper | 48 dp − / + buttons |
| OwnBrandBadge | Own brand |
| AppButton | "Add to cart" |

## Data fields shown

| Field | Source | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Store | `GET /v1/stores/nearest?farmer_id=` (remembered offline) | Kondapur Agri Store |
| Products | `GET /v1/catalog/products?store_id=` cached in the catalogue tables (`CatalogCache`) | Neem Shakti 500 ml ₹420.00 |
| Stock | Store snapshot | 20 in stock at your store |

## Actions and navigation

| Action | Result |
|---|---|
| Category chip | Filters the list |
| Product | F30 detail |
| − / + | Quantity |
| Add to cart | Sets the quantity in the cart and opens F31 |

## States covered

| State | Behaviour |
|---|---|
| Offline | Cached catalogue |
| Never loaded | `catalogNeverLoaded` |
| Empty category | `catalogEmpty` + `kisanShopEmptyHelp` |
| Out of stock | Red `kisanOutOfStock` |

## Accessibility and literacy

Every icon is paired with a text label; targets are at least 48 dp; body text 16 sp or larger; the key instruction has an `AudioButton` next to it (NF-009: device TTS in the chosen language behind the `AudioPrompt` seam, "Audio unavailable" when the phone has no voice for it). No text input (NF-010). At most two decisions on the screen.
