# C82 Campaign results

Surface: Web console (1440x900, responsive check 1024x768), Light and Dark. Route `/campaigns/{id}`. Owners, directors, tenant admins; auditors read.
Requirements: WC-008 (per-recipient delivery status), AN-008 (sent, delivered, orders, revenue, own-brand lift vs holdout).

Implementation: `apps/console/components/growth/CampaignResults.tsx` over `GET /v1/campaigns/{id}`, `GET /v1/campaigns/{id}/roi`, `GET /v1/campaigns/{id}/recipients?status=`, `POST /v1/campaigns/{id}/cancel`.

## Layout

1. Header: campaign name, channel and status chips, "Scheduled for 21 Sep 2026, 08:00", back link; **Cancel campaign** (destructive) while the campaign is a draft or scheduled.
2. A draft shows only "Results appear once the campaign has been sent."
3. Stat strip: Sent, Delivered, Failed (danger when non-zero), Opened (push) or Skipped, Orders (with the conversion rate underneath), Revenue, Own-brand share, **Own-brand lift vs holdout**, **Conversion lift vs holdout** (success when positive, danger when negative).
4. **Conversion window** note, always on screen: "an order counts when a farmer who received this campaign places it within 14 days of their message and no later campaign reached them first (last touch). The holdout arm is measured over the same window from the campaign start."
5. **Holdout** section: a grouped bar chart (SPEC 13.4 — bars for comparisons, no pies) of conversion and own-brand share, target arm vs holdout arm. Without a holdout arm a warning note asks for a holdout percentage before the next schedule.
6. **Recipients** table with a status filter: farmer, masked phone, arm (Receive the message / Holdout), language, status chip, sent at, reason (skip reason or delivery error).

## States

Loading: page skeleton, then a 40px stat skeleton while the ROI loads. Error: `QueryError` per query with retry. Cancel: confirmation dialog "Messages not yet sent are skipped. This cannot be undone."

## Copy keys

`campaigns.results.*` (including `recipientStatus.*` and `skipReason.*`), `campaigns.cancel|cancelTitle|cancelHelp|scheduledFor`, `campaigns.arms.*`.

## Tests

`e2e/growth.spec.ts` "C81 -> C82" asserts the delivery stats, the lift tiles, the conversion-window text and the holdout comparison.
