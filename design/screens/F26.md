# F26 Result — provisional

Surface: Farmer app "Kisan" (Android, 360x800 dp), Light and Sunlight themes.
Requirements: FM-005 acceptance ("provisional — expert will confirm" and notifies on update), SPEC 3.4 (no buy button until confirmed; "talk to your field officer" instead), DX-006. Derived from SPEC 13.3 F26.

Implementation: same screen as F25 (`DiagnosisResultScreen`) when `provisional` / `can_buy` false.

## Behaviour

- Headline "Provisional — expert will confirm"; the result card has the dashed border, the band in words (Medium confidence / Provisional) and "Our expert will confirm within 24 hours".
- Treatments are still shown (dose, PHI, PPE, safety text: the farmer can talk them through with the officer) but **no buy action anywhere**: no Order in the bar, the product cards have no button (`ProductCard.showAction: false`), and the order path refuses a non-buyable diagnosis.
- Bottom bar: **Talk to your field officer** (dials the officer) and **Ask an expert** (already with the agronomist → "Our expert already has your photos").
- When the agronomist confirms or corrects, the change arrives in the sync feed → F27.
