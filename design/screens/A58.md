# A58 Khata for farmer

Surface: Field agent app "Mitra", Light and Sunlight, 360x800.
Requirements: FA-022 (balance equals the sum of ledger entries; due dates linked to harvest month; payment history).

Implementation: `screens/khata/khata_screen.dart` (`KhataScreen`, `KhataBody` also used as the A22 Khata tab) over `khataProvider` (`KhataRepository`, Drift `khata_snapshots`). Route `/farmers/:id/khata`.

## Layout

1. `khataAsOf(time)` or, offline, `khataOfflineAsOf(time)`.
2. KhataLedger (key `khata-ledger`): balance = sum of the entries shown (every page is fetched), next due date, `khataOverdue`, bucket pills 0–30 / 31–60 / 61–90 / 90+ (`khata-bucket-<wire>`), entries debit / credit with `khataDueHarvest(date, month)`, Collect → A52 dues.
3. `khataPendingCollections`: this week's dues collections not yet on the ledger (waiting to sync / pending reconciliation).
4. `khataPaymentHistory` (`khataNoPayments`).
5. Not cached and offline: `khata-not-loaded` (`khataNotLoaded` / `khataNotLoadedHelp`).

## Behaviour

Refreshed on open when online (pull to refresh too).

Every state has an offline banner (`OfflineBannerLive`); strings are ARB keys in all five languages; money via `formatInr`.
