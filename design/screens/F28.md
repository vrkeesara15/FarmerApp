# F28 Not a plant / unsupported crop

Surface: Farmer app "Kisan" (Android, 360x800 dp), Light and Sunlight themes.
Requirements: DX-010 (friendly message, routed to the expert), FM-006 (ask an expert). Derived from SPEC 13.3 F28.

Implementation: `DiagnosisResultScreen` when `status == out_of_scope`.

## Behaviour

- One card: pictogram (no-photo for not a plant, question mark otherwise), the title for `out_of_scope_reason` — "We could not see a plant in the photo", "We cannot check this crop yet", "The photo was not clear enough", "We could not find this problem", "Our expert could not tell from the photos" — and "Our expert will look at it. You can also call your field officer."
- No problem, no products, no buy action.
- Bottom bar: **Ask an expert** (the case is already in the agronomist queue → "Our expert already has your photos") and **Talk to your field officer**.
- The on-device gate (DX-001 `not_plant`) already stops most of these before upload; F28 covers what the server finds.
