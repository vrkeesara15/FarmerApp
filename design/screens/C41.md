# C41 Store detail

Surface: Web console, Light and Dark. Route `/stores/{id}` (+ filters; a store / cluster filter from C40 does not apply here).
Requirements: WC-004 acceptance "Store P&L view by month", AN-005. Roles as C40 (store manager: own store only, others 404).

Implementation: `apps/console/app/(console)/stores/[id]/StoreDetailView.tsx`. Data: `useStoreDetail` (filter range) and `useStorePnl` (the 12 months ending at the filter's end day, `pnlWindow`) over `GET /v1/analytics/stores/{id}`, fetched in parallel.

## Layout

1. Back link "Stores"; header store name, code · district, range.
2. FilterBar without store / cluster; export = CSV of the P&L by month.
3. Summary StatTiles: net sales, cost of goods, gross margin, margin %, own-brand share, stock value, stock turns (annualised).
4. "Sales by category" horizontal bars (net sales vs gross margin per category, `categories.*`); "Own brand vs multi-brand" bars (net sales Turmeric, margin muted; InfoTip own-brand share).
5. "Credit book ageing" bars by bucket (0–30, 31–60, 61–90, 90+ days) + "₹X outstanding, ₹Y overdue across N accounts"; "Stock" (value at cost, SKUs in stock, turns in period, annualised, stockouts list); "Day-close variance" bars per day (danger when flagged) + "Show day closes as a table" (date, cash expected, counted, variance with Flagged badge, status).
6. "P&L by month" (`pnl-table`, 12 months: "12 months: 01-10-2025 – 18-09-2026"): rows gross sales, discounts, net sales, cost of goods sold, gross margin, gross margin %, write-offs, contribution; months cut by the window marked "partial"; negative values in danger colour. Note under the table (`pnl-note`): "Operating expenses (rent, salaries, power) are not included: contribution is gross margin less write-offs." (the API's `pnl_opex_included: false`).

## States

Loading skeletons per section; empty `storeDetail.noSalesTitle`, `noDayClosesTitle`; P&L error with retry inside the section; 404 / 403 as C10.

## Copy keys

`storeDetail.*`, `stores.columns.*`, `categories.*`, `buckets.*`, `brand.*`.

## Tests

`e2e/analytics.spec.ts` "C40 -> C41 store detail shows the P&L by month" (13 header cells, every P&L line, the note); `lib/mock/analytics.test.ts` (P&L identities, partial months).
