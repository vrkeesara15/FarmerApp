# A53 UPI QR

Surface: Field agent app "Mitra", Light and Sunlight, 360x800.
Requirements: FA-021 (UPI dynamic QR; the Razorpay webhook marks paid).

Implementation: `screens/payments/upi_screen.dart`; `UpiPoller` (`packages/core/lib/src/payments/upi_poller.dart`). Route `/payments/:id/upi`.

## Layout

1. Farmer name; UpiQrPanel (key `upi-panel-<state>`) with the provider `qr_payload` rendered by `qr_flutter` (black on white), amount, countdown `upiExpiresIn`.
2. States: waiting (`upiWaiting`, `upiScanHint`), paid (`upiPaid`), failed (`upiFailed`, or `upiExpired` after expiry) with `upiRetry` (new QR).
3. Bottom: paid → `paymentDone` (A57 or A58); otherwise `upiCollectCash` → A55 for the same target.

## Behaviour

Polls `GET /v1/payments/{id}` every 3 s for 10 polls, then ×1.5 up to 10 s, and stops at `expires_at`; the final state is written to the local payment (server wins on the next pull). Online only: A52 never offers it offline.

Every state has an offline banner (`OfflineBannerLive`); strings are ARB keys in all five languages; money via `formatInr`.
