# F61 Rating

Surface: Farmer app "Kisan" (Android, 360x800 dp), Light and Sunlight themes.
Requirements: FM-011 (P0). Derived from SPEC 13.3 (F61), 13.1 components and SPEC 2.4 / 7.

Implementation: `apps/mobile/apps/farmer/lib/screens/visit/visit_rating_screen.dart` (route `/visit/<visitId>/rate`, token in `extra`).

## Purpose

Five faces (1–5, each with a word) and quick reason chips (good: useful advice, looked at my crop, came on time; poor: visit too short, did not see my crop, advice not clear). "Send" posts the token with the rating and reason to `POST /v1/visits/{id}/farmer-confirm` (stored against the visit); "Confirm without rating" sends the token alone. Offline the confirmation is queued as a `visit_confirmation` create (the server validates the token on arrival). Two decisions.

## Accessibility and literacy

Every icon is paired with a text label; targets are at least 48 dp; body text 16 sp or larger; the key instruction has an `AudioButton` next to it (NF-009: device TTS in the chosen language behind the `AudioPrompt` seam, "Audio unavailable" when the phone has no voice for it). No text input (NF-010). At most two decisions on the screen.
