# F60 Visit confirmation prompt

Surface: Farmer app "Kisan" (Android, 360x800 dp), Light and Sunlight themes.
Requirements: FM-011 (P0) with FA-016. Derived from SPEC 13.3 (F60), 13.1 components and SPEC 2.4 / 7.

Implementation: `apps/mobile/apps/farmer/lib/screens/visit/visit_confirm_screen.dart` (route `/visit/<visitId>`) and `visit_scan_screen.dart` (route `/visit-scan`).

## Purpose

"Did Ramesh visit your farm today?" with Yes / No, read aloud. The prompt comes from a push payload carrying the signed token (FA-007 delivery calls `KisanRepository.receivePush`) or from a pending `app_push` confirmation in the sync feed (no token). With a token, Yes goes to F61; without, Yes goes to the QR scan: the officer shows the A32 QR, the farmer scans it (`mobile_scanner`, unbundled ML Kit model) and the token names the visit.

## Data fields shown

| Field | Source | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Officer | Push payload `officer_name` | Ramesh |
| Token | Push payload or scanned QR (JWT claims `vid`, `cid`, `ch`, `exp` read on the phone, verified by the server) | eyJ… |

## Actions and navigation

| Action | Result |
|---|---|
| Yes (token) | F61 |
| Yes (no token) | QR scan → F61 |
| No | Prompt hidden; "Thank you. We will check with the officer."; F10 |
| Scan a non-visit QR | `kisanScanNotVisitQr` |

## States covered

| State | Behaviour |
|---|---|
| Already answered | `kisanVisitAlreadyAnswered` |
| Expired prompt | Not shown |

## Accessibility and literacy

Every icon is paired with a text label; targets are at least 48 dp; body text 16 sp or larger; the key instruction has an `AudioButton` next to it (NF-009: device TTS in the chosen language behind the `AudioPrompt` seam, "Audio unavailable" when the phone has no voice for it). No text input (NF-010). At most two decisions on the screen.
