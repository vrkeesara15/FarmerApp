# F24 Analysing

Surface: Farmer app "Kisan" (Android, 360x800 dp), Light and Sunlight themes.
Requirements: FM-004 / FM-005, NF-002 / NF-003 (offline queue, resumable uploads). Derived from SPEC 13.3 "F24 Analysing (with offline "we'll check when you're online")".

Implementation: waiting state of `DiagnosisResultScreen` (`apps/mobile/apps/farmer/lib/screens/crop_doctor/diagnosis_result_screen.dart`, route `/crop-doctor/result/<id>`).

## Purpose

Say what is happening while the answer is on its way, and that nothing is lost offline.

## Behaviour

- Online: spinner, "Checking your crop…", "This takes a few seconds" (read aloud). `DiagnosisPoller` pushes the outbox (uploads first, then the create op), then polls `GET /v1/diagnoses/{id}` every 2 s backing off to 10 s; locally the answer comes in the applied echo, in stg / prd by the poll or the sync feed. The screen switches to F25 / F26 / F28 by itself.
- Offline (`key diagnosis-queued`): cloud-off icon, "Saved on your phone", "We’ll check when you’re online". The photos stay on the phone (`DocumentsPhotoStore`) and upload in 256 KB chunks when the network returns (on start, on reconnect, or the next poll).
- Refused by the server (e.g. `diagnosis_image_missing`): "Could not send your photos. Take them again."
