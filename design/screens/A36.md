# A36 Demo plot

Surface: Field agent app "Mitra" (Android, 360x800 dp), Light and Sunlight. From Me → Demo plots. Routes `/demo-plots` and `/demo-plots/{id}`.
Requirements: FA-026 (register a demo, track stages with photos, compare to farmer practice, yield entry), NF-004 (image budget), FA-025 (offline), FA-005 (voice on the treatment and the observation note).

Implementation: `apps/mobile/apps/agent/lib/screens/field/demo_plots_screen.dart` (`DemoPlotsScreen`, `NewDemoPlotSheet`, `DemoPlotScreen`, `AddObservationSheet`, `YieldSheet`) over `demoPlotsProvider` / `demoPlotProvider(id)` (`FieldRepository.watchDemoPlots`, the `demo_plot` and `demo_observation` sync entities).

## List (`/demo-plots`)

AppBar `demoTitle`, FAB `demoNew` (key `demo-new`), one card per plot (key `demo-<id>`) with the treatment, the farmer and `demoStatus*`. Empty: `demoEmpty` / `demoEmptyHelp` (key `demo-empty`).

**New demo plot** sheet: farmer chips (`demo-farmer-<id>`), crop-plot chips (`demo-plot-<id>`) from that farmer's farms, a `VoiceTextField` treatment (`demo-treatment`) and baseline chips (`demo-baseline-farmer_practice|comparison_plot`).

## Detail (`/demo-plots/{id}`)

1. Treatment, farmer, `demoStatus*` (key `demo-status`), `fieldPendingSync` while queued.
2. Comparison card (key `demo-comparison`) once a yield exists: demo vs farmer practice and `demoYieldGain(pct)` (key `demo-gain`).
3. Observations, oldest first (key `demo-observation-<id>`): stage, note, date or `fieldPendingSync`, and the photo count.
4. `demoAddObservation` (key `demo-add-observation`), disabled once the plot is `reported`.
5. Lifecycle buttons for the legal next states only (key `demo-transition-<state>`): `demoStart` from `created`, `demoReport` from `harvested`. `demoEnterYield` (key `demo-enter-yield`) shows while `active`.

**Observation** sheet: stage chips (`demo-stage-<stage>`), side chips (`demo-side-demo|practice|both`), `demoTakePhoto` (key `demo-take-photo`, NF-004 pipeline, count at `demo-photo-count`) and a `VoiceTextField` note (`demo-note`). Each photo becomes a resumable upload with the `demo_plot` purpose and the observation op carries `upload:<id>` placeholders.

**Yield** sheet: `demoYieldDemo` (key `demo-yield-demo`) and `demoYieldPractice` (key `demo-yield-practice`) in kg per acre; saving moves the plot to `harvested` and the gain is computed locally until the server sends its own.

Lifecycle: `created → active → harvested → reported`. Anything else is refused on the device as well as on the server (409 `demo_plot_transition_invalid`).
