# F32 Pay

Surface: Farmer app "Kisan" (Android, 360x800 dp), Light and Sunlight themes.
Requirements: FM-010 (P0) with NF-010. Derived from SPEC 13.3 (F32), 13.1 components and SPEC 2.4 / 7.

Implementation: `apps/mobile/apps/farmer/lib/screens/orders/pay_screen.dart` (route `/cart/pay`), `widgets/upi_pay.dart` (`payByUpi`).

## Purpose

One decision: Pay by UPI (opens the phone's UPI apps), Pay at store, or Ask for credit ("your store will confirm"); then "Place order". The order is placed online (`POST /v1/orders`, a farmer ordering for themselves); UPI then creates a payment for the order and launches the `upi://pay` intent from the payments API, waits on a sheet while polling `GET /v1/payments/{id}` (3 s, backing off to 10 s, until the QR expiry).

## Actions and navigation

| Action | Result |
|---|---|
| UPI | Order placed → payment → UPI app → paid: F33 "Paid. Thank you!"; cancelled or failed: F33 "Not paid yet…" |
| Pay at store | Order placed with no payment mode → F33 "Pay at the store when you collect" |
| Ask for credit | `payment_mode: credit`: placed on the khata, or 402 `credit_limit_exceeded` (approval requested) → "Your store will confirm", F34 |

## States covered

| State | Behaviour |
|---|---|
| Offline | "This needs internet…" (the server prices and reserves stock); nothing is sent |
| Short stock | `kisanOrderShort` |

## Accessibility and literacy

Every icon is paired with a text label; targets are at least 48 dp; body text 16 sp or larger; the key instruction has an `AudioButton` next to it (NF-009: device TTS in the chosen language behind the `AudioPrompt` seam, "Audio unavailable" when the phone has no voice for it). No text input (NF-010). At most two decisions on the screen.
