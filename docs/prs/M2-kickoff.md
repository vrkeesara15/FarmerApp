Design decisions and assumptions:
- Native declarative partitioning with a SQL helper rather than pg_partman or Python-side DDL: the jobs run as the app role, which must never get DDL (SEC-011); a `SECURITY DEFINER` function owned by the table owner is the smallest privilege that lets a scheduled job create tomorrow's partition. Policies are installed on every child because Postgres does not inherit RLS policies from the parent (queries through the parent use the parent's policy; direct child access uses the child's). Both paths are tested.
- Retention floor of 180 days is enforced inside the function, so even a compromised app role cannot purge sooner.
- `location_point.officer_id` has no FK (partition-wide FK checks on every officer change); `duty_session_id` likewise. The parent's indexes are inherited by each partition.
- Track days are IST calendar days (officers work IST days); partitions are UTC days, so a day spans at most two partitions. `location_daily_agg.day` is the UTC partition day.
- Visits are not on the agronomist's or accountant's routes (SPEC 2.6 has no column for them; notes are not crop data). A farmer sees visits made to their own record. Recorded as an assumption, easy to widen.
- `OVERRIDE_POINTS = 15` (of the geofence 30) for an approved override is an assumption; the SPEC only defines the five weights.
- `consent_version` moved into `TenantSettings` for documentation only; `farmers/service.py` keeps reading the raw key (unchanged behaviour).
- The seed's location paths are straight lines between the store and farm centroids with jitter (no road snapping — VV-006 is P1 and later).
