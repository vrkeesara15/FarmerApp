## Definition of done — status
- **Corpus answers ≥ 70 % of pilot test set**: not measured. The pipeline and evaluation script exist, but there is no agronomist-labelled field test set and the corpus images are synthetic.
- **Farmer app in Play closed testing**: not done; Play Console release is a human gate.

## Human gates
- Real field images for the corpus and an agronomist-labelled held-out test set; retune per-crop thresholds with the real encoder.
- Licence review of SigLIP and the ImageNet-pretrained MobileNetV2 weights for commercial use.
- `ANTHROPIC_API_KEY` in Secret Manager; deploy `embed` to dev and confirm reachability.
- On-device model under 400 ms on a 2 GB reference phone, then field validation before enabling the crop guess.
- Native-speaker review of new strings and recorded audio prompts.
- Play Console closed testing of the farmer app.
